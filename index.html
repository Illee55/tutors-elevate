About

A small team. A serious commitment to your child's progress.

Big agencies treat students like a number. Most private tutors lack structure. We built a small, specialist team: every tutor knows their subject deeply, every lesson is planned, every parent knows how their child is doing.

1

Real understanding beats memorising tricks.

We teach the why behind every method. Students who understand it remember it under exam pressure. The ones who only memorised it forget.

2

Patience is the most underrated skill in teaching.

Confidence is built one small win at a time. We make sure your child gets it, not just nods along.

3

Honesty serves students better than flattery.

We tell parents what's actually happening: what's strong, what's wobbly, what we're doing about it. No glossy reports.

4

Steady growth wins.

We don't cram. We want your child more confident in six weeks than today, and again the six weeks after that.

How it works

From "we're thinking about a tutor" to "they've actually improved."

Five steps. Here's exactly what happens.

1

Free 15-min consultation

A quick call. You tell us about your child: year, exam board, struggles, goals. We tell you honestly if we can help.

2

Free trial lesson

We match your child to a specialist tutor and run a free trial. No card details, no obligation.

3

Lessons begin

Weekly lessons start, usually within 7 days. Each lesson is planned in advance with tailored homework.

4

Progress reports

A brief written update every 4–6 weeks: what's clicked, what we're working on, what's next.

5

Results

Grade improvements, confidence built, exam goals hit. We track it so you can see the progress.

What we don't do.

  • Cram without building understanding
  • Show up without a plan
  • Give vague "they're doing well" updates
  • Lock you into a long-term contract
  • Send a different tutor each week
  • Ignore parents
