The terms that apply when you book lessons with us.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version

Your agreement is with us, for lessons you book. There's no long-term contract. Prices are on our website and on your invoice. You can cancel within 14 days of booking, and you can stop at any time after that with a pro-rata refund for unused lessons you've paid for. These terms don't affect your legal rights as a consumer.

1. About these terms

Tutors Elevate provides online tuition. In these pages, "we", "us" and "our" mean Tutors Elevate Ltd. Our details are: Tutors Elevate Ltd · Registered in England and Wales, company number 17394433 · Email Ilham@tutorselevate.co.uk · Phone 07467 218151.

These terms apply when you book tuition with us. Please read them with our refund and cancellation policy and our privacy notice. If the student is under 18, the agreement is with their parent or guardian, who is responsible for these terms and for payment.

If you book a class we run at a partner centre (such as SEM Education), we'll tell you at booking if any of that centre's own terms also apply.

2. Consultations, free trials and booking

  • The 15-minute consultation and the first trial lesson are free and you are under no obligation to book.
  • A booking is made, and these terms apply, when we confirm the lesson or class details to you in writing (by email, text or WhatsApp).
  • We'll agree the subject, format, day and time with you. We'll try to keep the same tutor throughout. If we ever need to change your tutor, we'll tell you first.

3. Fees and payment

  • Our current prices are on our pricing section. The price that applies to you is the one we confirm when you book and show on your invoice.
  • We send invoices by email or WhatsApp. Payment is by bank transfer to the account on the invoice, by the due date shown. Group blocks and some classes are paid in advance, before the first lesson.
  • We'll give you at least 30 days' notice of any price change. If you don't want to continue at the new price, you can stop before it applies.
  • We are not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added to our prices.
  • If an invoice is unpaid 14 days after its due date, we may pause lessons until it is paid. We'll always contact you first.

4. Lessons

  • Online lessons take place over Google Meet. You need a reliable internet connection and a device with a microphone. A camera, and a quiet space, help a lot.
  • If our connection fails and the lesson can't continue, we'll add the lost time to another lesson or refund it. If the student's connection fails, we'll try to reconnect but can't always make up the time.
  • We will teach with reasonable care and skill, plan lessons in advance and set homework where it helps. We can't guarantee particular grades or results, because these depend on many things outside our control, including the student's own work and the exam itself.
  • Our progress updates, homework and resources are part of the service described on our website.

5. Safeguarding and conduct

  • The safety of children comes first. If we're worried a child may be at risk of harm, we will act on it, which may include contacting the relevant authorities.
  • For students under 18, a parent or guardian should be at home during online lessons.
  • Tutors contact students only through the channels agreed with the parent, and never through personal social media.
  • We expect students and tutors to treat each other with respect. We may end lessons if a student or parent is abusive, after warning you where reasonable, and refund any lessons paid for but not taken.
  • Group lessons may be recorded for catch-up, as explained in our privacy notice. Please don't record lessons yourself without our agreement and the agreement of others in the lesson.

6. Our materials

The worksheets, notes, checklists, recordings and other materials we create are ours. You may use them for the student's own learning, but not share them publicly, resell them or use them to teach others without our permission.

Past exam papers and mark schemes belong to the exam boards. AQA, Edexcel (Pearson), OCR and other names are trademarks of their owners. We are not connected to or endorsed by any exam board.

7. Ending lessons

  • There is no minimum term. You can stop lessons at any time by telling us. You won't be charged for lessons after that, and any lessons you've paid for in advance but not taken will be refunded pro-rata. Lessons that have already taken place are charged and not refundable. See our refund and cancellation policy.
  • We may stop providing lessons by giving you reasonable notice, and we'll refund any lessons paid for but not taken.

9. Complaints

If you're unhappy with anything, please tell us first at Ilham@tutorselevate.co.uk. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 5 working days and aim to resolve it within 14 days.

We try to keep this website accurate and up to date, but it is for general information. The topic checklists and grade boundary pages are free for personal and educational use.
