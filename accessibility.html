Accessibility statement

Our commitment to making this website usable for everyone.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or voice control. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've done

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Text and buttons meet WCAG colour contrast requirements.
  • Every form field has a label, required fields are marked, and error messages are read out by screen readers.
  • Images have text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Pages use headings and landmarks so screen-reader users can move around easily.
  • No content pops up by itself, flashes, or auto-plays.
  • We test pages with the axe accessibility checker.

Known issues

  • The printable topic checklists and grade boundary pages are designed for printing on A4. They work on screen but are best printed or zoomed.

Accessible lessons

We can adapt lessons and materials, for example with large-print worksheets. Tell us what would help on your free consultation.
