How we teach

The philosophy behind every lesson.

Four principles that every Tutors Elevate lesson is built on.

1

Real understanding beats memorising tricks.

We teach the why behind every method. Students who understand it remember it under exam pressure. The ones who only memorised it forget.

2

Patience is the most underrated skill in teaching.

Confidence is built one small win at a time. We make sure your child gets it, not just nods along.

3

Honesty serves students better than flattery.

We tell parents what's actually happening: what's strong, what's wobbly, what we're doing about it. No glossy reports.

4

Steady growth wins.

We don't cram. We want your child more confident in six weeks than today, and again the six weeks after that.

Standards

How we choose and develop our tutors.

In-house training before the first lesson.

Every tutor completes an intensive in-house training programme before they teach their first student. That means lesson planning structure, how to adapt to different learning styles, how to give feedback that students and parents actually find useful, and how to handle the moments when a student is stuck and frustrated.

✓

Top grades in their subject.

We don't recruit anyone who hasn't achieved at the highest level in what they teach. You can't teach what you don't know deeply.

✓

Years of teaching experience.

All our tutors have taught the same subjects across multiple year groups for multiple years. Experience in the classroom is different from knowing the content.

✓

Peer review of lesson plans.

Tutors review each other's lesson plans regularly. It keeps standards up and stops habits forming that students eventually notice.

✓

Direct student feedback every term.

We survey students independently, not through the tutor. The results go back to every tutor and to us. That's how the 97% recommendation rate gets maintained.
