Privacy notice

How we collect, use and protect information about you and your child.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version

1. Who we are

Tutors Elevate provides online tuition. In these pages, "we", "us" and "our" mean Tutors Elevate Ltd. Our details are: Tutors Elevate Ltd · Registered in England and Wales, company number 17394433 · Email Ilham@tutorselevate.co.uk · Phone 07467 218151.

We are the controller of the personal information described in this notice, which means we decide how it is used and are responsible for looking after it under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

3. Health and special educational needs

Sometimes a parent tells us about a student's health, disability or special educational needs (for example, a visual impairment that means they need large-print worksheets) so that we can adapt lessons. This is "special category" information and the law gives it extra protection.

We only record it if you choose to tell us and give your explicit consent for us to use it to adapt the student's lessons. We share it only with the student's tutor. You can withdraw consent at any time. Please don't put this kind of information in the website enquiry form; tell us on the call instead.

4. Lesson recordings

We may record online group sessions so that students can catch up on what they missed or revise. We do not record one-to-one lessons unless you ask us to.

We tell everyone at the start of a session if it is being recorded. Students can keep their camera off and use the chat instead of speaking. Recordings are shared only with students in that group and their parents, are not published, and are deleted within 90 days. If you don't want your child to appear in a recording, tell us and we'll make sure they don't.

We rely on our legitimate interest in providing catch-up material to the group. You have the right to object, and we will respect that.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only where we need to. We use these service providers, who act on our instructions:

  • Formspree (United States): receives the enquiry form and delivers it to our email.
  • Google (Google Workspace): our business email, documents, feedback survey forms, and Google Meet, which we use for online lessons.
  • GitHub (United States): hosts this website (see This website).
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.
  • Our bank and accountant, for payments and accounts.

We also share information with:

  • The tutor who teaches your child. Some of our tutors are self-employed and work with us under a written agreement to keep your information confidential and use it only for teaching.
  • SEM Education, if your child was introduced to us by SEM or attends a class we run at an SEM centre. We share the student's name, attendance and fees so that SEM can manage its classes and referral commission.
  • HMRC and other authorities where the law requires it.
  • Children's services or the police if we believe a child is at risk of harm. Protecting children comes first, and we may do this without telling you first if telling you could put the child at risk.

6. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our providers (Formspree, GitHub, Google and Meta) may store or process information in the United States. Where this happens we rely on the protections the law allows, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum included in the provider's data processing terms. You can ask us for more detail.

8. Children and young people

Most of our students are under 18. We collect information about them from their parent or guardian, and the parent or guardian is the person we make the agreement with. We don't knowingly accept enquiries through our website from anyone under 18 unless a parent or guardian is involved.

If you're a student: we keep your first name, year, subjects and how you're getting on so your tutor can plan your lessons. Only your tutor, your parent or guardian and the Tutors Elevate team see it, and we delete it when you stop having lessons with us. We never put your name on our website. If you're worried about anything, or want to see what we hold about you, you or your parent can email us. Young people can usually make these requests themselves from around age 13 (12 in Scotland).

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you or your child;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete information;
  • ask us to limit how we use it;
  • object to us using it where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • ask for information you gave us in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent. This doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

Email Ilham@tutorselevate.co.uk. We will reply within one month. Some rights have legal limits (for example, we must keep invoices for tax), and we'll explain if one applies.

10. Tutor applications

If you apply to teach with us, we use your CV, covering note and anything you share at interview to decide whether to offer you work, relying on taking steps before a contract with you. If we offer you work, we'll also ask for proof of your right to work and, because you will be teaching children, carry out appropriate safeguarding checks such as a DBS check. We'll tell you more at that point. Unsuccessful applications are deleted after 6 months.

11. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or social media plug-ins, and it stores nothing on your device. Our fonts are served from our own site. See our cookie policy.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub records technical information such as your IP address when you visit, to deliver the site and keep it secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Links to WhatsApp and other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

12. Security

We keep information in password-protected accounts with two-step sign-in where available, and only share it with people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong that puts your information at risk, we will tell you and, where required, the Information Commissioner's Office.

13. Questions and complaints

If you have a question or are unhappy with how we've handled your information, email Ilham@tutorselevate.co.uk. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you what we're doing about it.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

14. Changes to this notice

We'll update this notice if the way we use information changes, and change the date at the top. If a change affects you significantly, we'll tell you directly.
