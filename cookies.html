Cookie policy

Our website doesn't use cookies. Here's what that means.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version

This website doesn't use cookies, and it doesn't store anything else on your device either. That's why we don't show you a cookie banner.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), a website must ask your permission before using them, unless they are strictly necessary or fall within a small number of other exemptions.

2. What this website uses

TypeUsed on this site?
Strictly necessary cookiesNo
Analytics or statistics (e.g. Google Analytics)No
Advertising or tracking pixelsNo
Social media plug-ins or embedded videosNo
Local or session storageNo

Our fonts are served from our own website rather than a third-party font service, so loading a page doesn't send your details to anyone else.

3. Other services

  • Hosting. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which does not set cookies on this site but does record technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect it. See our privacy notice.
  • Enquiry form. When you send the form, your answers go to Formspree, which delivers them to us. Sending the form doesn't set any cookies.
  • Links. If you follow a link to WhatsApp or another website, that site's own cookie policy applies.

4. If this changes

If we ever add analytics or anything else that stores information on your device and needs your permission, we'll update this page and ask for your consent first, with an equally easy option to say no.
