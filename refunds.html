Refund and cancellation policy

How cancellations and refunds work, including your 14-day right to cancel.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version

  • You can cancel any new booking within 14 days and get your money back, less the cost of any lessons you asked us to start in that time.
  • One-to-one lessons: cancel at least 24 hours before and there's no charge.
  • Lessons that have already taken place are charged and can't be refunded.
  • Paid in advance and want to stop? We refund lessons you haven't had yet.
  • If we cancel, you choose: a rescheduled lesson or a full refund.

1. Your 14-day right to cancel

Because you book with us by email, phone, WhatsApp or online, the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 give you the right to cancel within 14 days, without giving a reason. The 14 days start on the day we confirm your booking.

If you want lessons to start within those 14 days, we'll ask you to confirm that in writing. If you then cancel, you'll pay for the lessons you've already had and get the rest back. If you asked us to start early, confirmed you understood this, and all the lessons in the booking have already been given, the right to cancel no longer applies.

To cancel, tell us clearly by email to Ilham@tutorselevate.co.uk, or by WhatsApp. You can use the form at the bottom of this page, but you don't have to. We'll refund you within 14 days of you telling us, by bank transfer to the account you paid from.

2. One-to-one lessons

  • 24 hours' notice or more: no charge. We'll rebook the lesson or, if you've paid, refund it or carry it forward, as you prefer.
  • Less than 24 hours' notice, or not turning up: the lesson is charged in full, because the tutor has kept the time free and prepared for it. If we can fill the slot or move the lesson to another time that week, we won't charge.
  • Illness or emergencies: please tell us as soon as you can. We'll use our discretion and won't charge unreasonably.
  • If the student is more than 15 minutes late and hasn't told us, the tutor may treat the lesson as missed.

3. Group classes and blocks paid in advance

  • Group classes and blocks are paid in advance, before the first lesson.
  • Missing a single group lesson: the class goes ahead for the rest of the group, so a single missed lesson isn't refunded. Where we have them, we'll share the notes and any recording so the student can catch up.
  • Stopping a block early: tell us before the next lesson and we'll refund the lessons that haven't happened yet, pro-rata.
  • Discounts: if you had a block or sibling discount, the pro-rata refund is calculated at the discounted rate you paid.

4. If we cancel or can't deliver a lesson

If we cancel a lesson, or it can't go ahead because of a problem on our side (for example, our connection fails), you choose: a rescheduled lesson at a time that suits you, or a full refund for that lesson.

5. Free consultation and trial lesson

These are free. There's nothing to pay and nothing to cancel.

6. Lessons that have taken place

Once a lesson has taken place it is charged and is not refundable. If you feel a lesson fell short, tell us within 7 days and we'll offer to repeat it free of charge.

This doesn't affect your legal rights. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, if a lesson wasn't delivered with reasonable care and skill and can't be put right by repeating it, you may be entitled to a price reduction.

7. How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid by bank transfer, to the account you paid from where possible, within 14 days of us agreeing the refund. There's no fee.
